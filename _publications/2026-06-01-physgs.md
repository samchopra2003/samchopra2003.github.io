PhysGS extends 3D Gaussian Splatting to estimate physical properties alongside a scene’s geometry and appearance. It combines visual evidence with vision-language priors and represents uncertainty in the estimated properties. The paper evaluates the method on objects and on indoor and outdoor scenes.
