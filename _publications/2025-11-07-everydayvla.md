EveryDayVLA pairs a low-cost six-degree-of-freedom manipulator with a policy that predicts both discrete and continuous actions. An adaptive-horizon ensemble uses action uncertainty to decide when to replan. The project includes a data collection pipeline and demonstrates the policy on real manipulation tasks.
